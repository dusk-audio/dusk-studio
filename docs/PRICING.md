# Pricing

This file is the single source of truth for Dusk Studio pricing. If the README,
the manual, the website or a Patreon post disagrees with this file, this file
wins.

During the beta, official builds come through
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/DuskAudio/membership).

## Stage

Beta. Patreon tiers are open now. Licences go on sale at 1.0.

## What you pay for

The source is always free on GitHub under GPL-3.0. Clone it, audit it, build
it, modify it, redistribute it. That will not change.

What you pay for is signed, ready-to-run builds: Linux tarball, Windows MSI and
macOS DMG, packaged and delivered, plus support access. Self-building is fully
supported and equivalent at the source level.

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Patreon Supporter | $5 / month | Official builds for all three OSes, new plugins and plugin updates two weeks early, credits, development updates |
| Patreon Patron | $10 / month | Everything in Supporter, plus early beta builds, roadmap votes, bug reports looked at first and direct messages on Patreon |
| One-time licence (from 1.0) | $49, planned | The current major version and all of its updates |
| Lifetime licence (from 1.0) | $89, planned | Every future major version |

$5 a month is the build floor for new members. Members of the closed $1 and
$3 tiers keep their build access. Below that there is no build, only source.

## Guarantee

30 days. Finish a track in your first session or full refund. No argument, no
form to fill in.

## Licence pricing

Licence prices are planned, not final. They are confirmed when licences go on
sale at 1.0.
